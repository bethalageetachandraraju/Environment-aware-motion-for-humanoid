Scene-aware motion on the G1

The robot gets one simple command, walk forward at a set speed (or stand still), and finds its own way past walls, cones, low ceilings and people. Each scene shows the same command twice: first the planner's motion played on the robot without physics, then the full system, where an RL tracking policy executes that motion under physics.

Free walking on a 2 m/s command.

Walking through a crowd

People walking at the robot

The robot is told to stand still; four people walk straight at it from four directions, 3.5 s apart. The Stage 2 clip is the first contact-free run of 8 recorded.

Wider corridor, two oncoming people

A 1.55 m corridor; two people walk the other way, 7 m apart, the first on the robot's left and the second on its right. The Stage 2 clip is the first contact-free run of 8 recorded.

Cone slalom to a half-open door

Cones in a 2.5 m corridor that ends at a partly closed door. Commanded at 1.0 m/s. The Stage 2 clip is the first of 12 recorded runs that reaches the door without contact; over 48 runs the full system reaches it in 21%.

Narrowing corridor

A corridor that narrows from 1.1 m to 0.39 m.

Dense cone field

Random clutter and people

Random cones, bollards, wall pieces and six people, different every run. The planner-only clip has the people but not the clutter, which is placed only in physics runs.
